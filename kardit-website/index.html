About Kardit

A Nigerian payments switch, built for the way Africa actually moves money.

Our Story

Founded in 2008 as a subsidiary of Chams Holdco Plc, Kardit is a multichannel electronic payments switch licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria. We are listed on the Visa Global Registry and a UnionPay International cross-border partner.

We are not the loudest fintech in Lagos. Our customers — banks, ministries, hospitals, and transit operators — choose us because the rails work, the audits pass, and the team picks up the phone.

Key Milestones

  • 2008: Founded and incorporated in Lagos
  • 2014: Licensed by CBN as payments switch
  • 2022: Listed on Visa Global Registry
  • 2024: UnionPay International licensee
